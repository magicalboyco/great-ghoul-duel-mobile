With Gratitude

Every little ghost has a story. This one exists because people imagined, composed, programmed, shared, taught, experimented, and helped one another. To all of you: thank you for making a tiny haunted world possible.

💖 Game Studio — Joy Haus

Joy Haus is the game studio behind this independent Spooktacular Showdown project. A little celebration of playful ideas, pastel ghosts, and people making things together.

🎃 To the original ghost makers

Google's 2018 Halloween Doodle, The Great Ghoul Duel, is the inspiration for the two-team spirit-flame game, power-ups, friendly competition, and end-of-match superlatives. Thank you to the Google Doodle and Google Cloud teams who brought that original experience to life.

Original art: Nate Swinehart · UX: Diana Tran · Production: Colin Duffy · Engineering: Tom Tabanao, Jonathan Shneier, Mark Ivey, Brian Murray, Ben McMahan, Jacob Howcroft.

Doodle leads: Jessica Yu, Brian Kaas, Ryan Germick · Marketing: Perla Campos, Carlos Diaz · Music and sound: aivi & surasshu, Stemage · Production support: My-Linh Le · Volunteer engineering: Brandon DeRosier, Monica Rodriguez, Katlyn Edwards.

Google Cloud: Micah Baker, Joseph Holley, Laith Massarweh, Shana Matthews, Nita Meng.

🧑‍💻 To the programmers and open-web community

Thank you to the creators of JavaScript, HTML, CSS, browser canvas, Web Audio, and the open tools and shared knowledge that make small independent games possible.

We also appreciate the independent Great Ghoul Duel multiplayer-server community reference and the experiments that help people study and preserve games. Its server code is not bundled in this build.

This game's playable single-page implementation and character drawings live in the Spooktacular Showdown source repository.

🎶 To the musicians and sound explorers

To composers, performers, sound designers, and the keepers of musical history: your creativity makes games feel alive. Thank you especially to artists who make music available in the public domain or through Creative Commons.

What's actually in this version: Moonlit Mischief, an original synthesized soundtrack and sound effects programmed with the browser's Web Audio API. No third-party music recording or public-domain / Creative Commons track is bundled in this build.

Specific licensed tracks, creators, source links, and licenses will be listed here if any are added. See the repository's ATTRIBUTIONS.md for the current provenance record.

🌈 To the pastel dreamers

Thank you to artists who made gentle, colorful fantasy worlds feel magical. Pastel cartoon aesthetics, including the cheerful color sensibility associated with My Little Pony, are a visual inspiration here — not a claim to its characters, artwork, or endorsement.

🤖 To the AI assistants — and the people behind them

Thank you to ChatGPT, creative coding assistants, the earlier Lovable prototype, and the many researchers, engineers, testers, and human creators whose work contributes to modern creative tools. Their assistance helped turn a game idea into something playable.

AI model training sources cannot be reliably inferred from this game's code. We won't invent a list of songs, artworks, or people allegedly included in any model's training data.

For every brave dash across the map, every daring spirit rescue, every silly ghost face, and every second chance: thank you for playing.
